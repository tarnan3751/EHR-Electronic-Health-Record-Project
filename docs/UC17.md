# UC 17 — Answer a patient's message

SwiftChart EHR · the expansion plan's step 5 (FR-18) · Tahseen Arnan · a proposed use case, not in the course document

## Clinician workflow

- **A patient writes.** When a patient sends a message from the portal, it appears in the inbox of every clinician who has them on their panel: their care team. The inbox item says only "A patient sent a message", with the patient's name and **Open the conversation**.
- **Read and reply.** The conversation shows the patient's banner and every message in order, each with who wrote it and when. Type a reply and choose **Send reply**. The item then leaves the inbox of everyone on the care team.
- **Start a conversation.** The chart's Messages section lists the patient's conversations, the latest first, and has **New message** for a patient on your panel.
- **The patient is told by email** that there is a new message, with a link to the portal. The email has no subject line from the conversation, no text and no names.

The patient's side is in `docs/UC6.md`.

## Decisions

- **Data.** `ehr.message_threads` (patient, subject, when started, when its latest message was sent) and `ehr.messages` (thread, patient, sender kind and account, text, when sent, when first read by the other side). Patient data: row-level security is enabled and forced, and the write-audit trigger records every write. Never edited or deleted.
- **Who.** A patient writes in their own threads. A clinician writes only for a patient on their active panel. Clinicians can read every thread in the database, as they can read every chart; the pages ask for the panel or emergency access first, and a clinician under emergency access reads without replying.
- **The database sets who and when.** A trigger sets a message's sender to the signed-in account and its time to the clock, whatever the client sent, and checks it belongs to its thread's patient. Row-level security also holds the sender kind to the account's own.
- **Read state.** A message is marked read once, by the other side only: the patient for a staff message, a care-team clinician for the patient's. The app role may update that one column.
- **The inbox.** A `SECURITY DEFINER` trigger puts a patient's message in each care-team clinician's inbox (one open item per thread each) and marks those items done when any staff message is sent in the thread. No runtime role can insert an inbox item. If nobody has the patient on their panel, there is no item: the conversation still shows on the chart.
- **Reads are audited.** Opening a conversation is a recorded read of the thread, for a clinician and for the patient, on the clinic's server and, through the cloud app's audit queue, off-site.
- **Admins never see the text.** The write audit keeps every message, but the audit page shows only that one was written and by whom (`ehr.shown_audit_details` leaves both tables out).
- **Email.** Sent after a staff message is saved, to the patient's confirmed portal address; a patient without a portal account gets none. One notice per unread run: while the patient has a staff message they haven't opened, further replies send no more email, so messaging can't use up the few emails an address may be sent in an hour, which password resets share.
- **Limits on a patient.** The database holds a patient to 10 new conversations a day and 30 messages an hour; past that the portal says "You've sent a lot of messages today. Call the clinic, or try again later." Staff aren't limited. The numbers are this step's choice.
- **Off-site.** Conversations are read from the cloud copy; sending is forwarded to the clinic's server. While that can't be reached the forms are replaced by "Messages can't be sent until the clinic's server is back." Read marks are only written on the clinic's server.
- **The portal takes a thread's ID in its address, never a patient's.** `PortalSubjectTests` allows that one ID by name, with a test that such a page takes its patient from the session.

## Limits

- No attachments, and no messages between staff.
- A conversation can't be closed or archived.
- The chart lists a patient's five latest conversations and the portal the latest 50; there is no page of older ones yet.
- A reply marks the thread's inbox items done for the team, including one for a patient message that arrived a moment before it.
- The notice email is queued when the reply is saved, a moment before its transaction commits.
- The portal's app bar doesn't count unread messages; the list marks conversations with something new.
- A patient with nobody on their care team can write, but nobody is notified.
- The desktop app doesn't show messages.
