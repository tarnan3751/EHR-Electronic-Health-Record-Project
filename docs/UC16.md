# UC 16 — Schedule an appointment

SwiftChart EHR · the expansion plan's step 4 (FR-17) · Tahseen Arnan · a proposed use case, not in the course document

## Clinician workflow

- **Book.** Open a patient on your panel and choose **Book appointment** in the chart's Appointments section. Pick the clinician (you, by default), the day, the time, the length and the reason for the visit, which the patient sees in the portal. The chart then lists the appointment.
- **See a day.** **Schedule** in the app bar shows your appointments for a day, earliest first, with each patient linked to their chart. Pick another clinician or day, or step a day back or forward. On a colleague's day, the reason for a visit is shown only for patients on your own panel.
- **Open an appointment** from the chart or the schedule to:
  - **Reschedule** it: a new day, time, length or reason;
  - **Cancel** it, with a reason, which is required;
  - **Check in** the patient when they arrive, which starts their visit and puts it on the chart;
  - **Mark as a no-show.**
- **Pre-visit forms** (the plan's step 13e, `docs/UC22.md`). The appointment's page links to the patient's pre-visit forms, where a clinician with the patient on their panel asks for a questionnaire before a booked appointment that hasn't ended. The patient can fill it in from 7 days before the appointment until its scheduled end, so a reschedule moves that window, and a cancellation or a no-show closes the form; one already answered stays with its appointment.
- An appointment's day and time are the clinic's (the plan's step 10d): typed on the clinic's clock, listed on the clinic's calendar day, and shown with the zone's abbreviation ("23:30 to 23:50 CST"). They are stored in UTC. A time the clocks skip on the day they go forward is refused; one that happens twice when they go back is taken as the first.

## Decisions

- **Who.** Clinicians, for a patient on their panel, with any clinician: the rule for orders and the chart's lists. Under emergency access an appointment can be read and nothing changed. Admins have no access to patient data, and there is no front-desk role, so neither schedules.
- **Data.** `ehr.appointments`: patient, clinician, start, length, reason, status (FHIR's `booked`, `cancelled`, `fulfilled`, `noshow`), the cancellation reason, the visit it became, who booked it and when. Patient data: row-level security is enabled and forced, and the write-audit trigger records every change. Never deleted.
- **The four moves.** A trigger (`db/policies/80-appointments.sql`) lets a booked appointment be rescheduled, cancelled with a reason, fulfilled by naming a visit of the same patient, or marked a no-show. After any of the last three it never changes again. The patient, the clinician and who booked it never change. The app role may update only the six columns those moves write.
- **No double booking.** The same trigger refuses a booked appointment that overlaps another of the same clinician. It locks the clinician's row first, so two bookings at the same moment can't both get through. The page then says the clinician is already booked and keeps what was entered.
- **Checking in creates the visit.** An encounter, in progress, with the appointment's reason, and the appointment names it, in one savepoint: both or neither. This is the first thing at runtime to write a visit, so the app role gained INSERT on `ehr.encounters`, for a patient on the clinician's panel only. Nothing updates or deletes a visit.
- **Changes name the version they began from.** Each appointment counts its changes, and a change from a page read before someone else's change is refused, with "changed by someone else first". So an old page can't put an old time back.
- **Only at READ COMMITTED.** The trigger refuses any other isolation level, as signing an order does: under a stricter one the overlap check would miss the booking it waited for.
- **A locked-out clinician still has a schedule.** Only a disabled account can't be booked with; one locked for minutes after wrong passwords can.
- **Reads.** The schedule names patients, so each patient on the day is one recorded read. The booking form and an appointment's page are each one recorded read of the patient. The chart's upcoming list is part of the chart's one read.
- **Off-site.** The schedule, the chart's list and the portal's pages are read from the cloud copy. Booking and changes are forwarded to the clinic's server. While that can't be reached, those are off the pages with a sentence saying why.
- **Desktop.** Appointments aren't in the offline copy.

## Limits

- No self-scheduling, reminders or waiting list.
- No working hours or time off: any time can be booked.
- The portal still shows a patient their appointments in UTC, labeled; only clinician pages use the clinic's clock so far.
- A visit that check-in starts is never finished by the app: it stays "in progress".
- A cancelled or missed appointment can't be reopened; book a new one.
